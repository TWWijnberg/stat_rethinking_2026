What stands out

Food and group size are close to collinear — r = 0.90. Any model carrying both is asking the data to separate two things it has barely separated.

Weight is almost entirely unexplained. All three territory variables together reach ~10% of its variation; food alone reaches 0.1%.

Food shows nothing against weight while group size shows a weak negative — the signature of two effects cancelling, not of no effect.

Only weight varies within a territory. Area, food and group size are territory properties repeated on every fox — and group size is the number of rows each territory contributes.
